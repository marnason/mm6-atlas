New Sorpigal: Gharik's Forge
Fire Elementals assisted Gharik in his attempts to create powerful items here. A handful of his powerfuls students continue his experiments.


Gharik's Forge
1. Entrance
2. There is a secret room here off to the side, and a switch to open the bridge
3. This takes time. After Killing all the Fire Elementals, you have to make your way to each of the 4 doors, and hit the swithces to make the bridge move so that you can get across on the other side.
4. Item the Hourglass of time from the [Find Hourglass Quest].
5. Letter to Marcus
6. The key for this door is in Silver Helm Outpost
7. There is about 10,000 gold down here, but there are a lof of the fire guy's down here.


This is a fairly tough one. You MUST have good fire protection and decent electrical protection if you plan on surviving for long at all.

A person that is good with water magic is highly suggested, as they can do the most damage to the fire elementals with Ice Bold, or Ice Blast. The Magicians you'll just have to tough it out with.
