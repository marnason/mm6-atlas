Castle Ironfist: Temple of Baa
Faint chanting can be heard from beyond the doors of this Temple. Visitors do not appear to be welcome here.


 1    Door Puzzle, open doors in this order: N,E,W,S
Note: Be prepared for an encounter with 20+ skelletons and 10 Magicans

 6  The store room. The Bathouse key is in here.
 7  The Bathhouse, The Store Room Key is in the chest.
 8  Store Room, Secret room key
 9  The second bathhouse the chest contains the key to the Treasure room at 3
Note: there is a hidden treasure in on of the torches between 4, and 2, in the hallway between them.
