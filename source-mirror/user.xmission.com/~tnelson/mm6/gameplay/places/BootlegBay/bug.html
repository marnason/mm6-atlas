Bootleg Bay: Hall of the Fire Lord BUG!
The Quest offered by the Fire Lord has a pretty nasty bug. Please do not read the following, unless you like to spoil the fun of the game.

Once you have completed the quest and unwarted the doors, go back to the Fire Lord and talk to him. Click on "Quest" and you will receive your experience points, if you now continue clicking on "Quest" over and over, you will receive your rewarts multiple times. This only works while talking for the first time to the Fire Lord after you completed the quest. As soon as you Exit the talk to the Fire Lord it won´t work again.
