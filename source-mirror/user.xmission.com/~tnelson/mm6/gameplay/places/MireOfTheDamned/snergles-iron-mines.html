Mire of the Damned: Snergle's Iron Mines
Recommended character level: 25+


1. Cell Key
2. Ghim Hammond, gives you the Key to Snergle's Chambers which is needed in Castle Ironfist in the Snergle's Caverns. The door is locked and requires cell key from #1.
3. Those cell doors open once you open the door at #2.
4. Room with about 15 spell books.


In this dungeon are a lot of Dwarfs, so you need some good armor protection. There are also a bunch of slime monsters, they are imune to physical damage, so use spells. On the top floor is a room with about 15 spell books, but you will most likely already have those spells.
